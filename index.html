How to use LDJ 3D Viewer:
• Drag with one finger to rotate the model
• Pinch with two fingers to zoom in and out
• Double-tap a point on the fossil to re-centre rotation there
• Use the toolbar icons to flip, cycle views, measure, slice, recolor, and view properties
• Tap 📝 to write notes, and 📖 (bottom) to read them anytime
• Tap ⚙ to reorder your toolbar icons
• Tap 📐 after measuring two points to set the fossil's real-world size
